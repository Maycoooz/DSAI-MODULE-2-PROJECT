Chapter 02 — Delivery Performance

Geography drives delivery speed

Sellers in SP, PR and MG deliver the fastest. Remote northern states like AM and RO see average delivery times exceeding 20 days. On-time delivery rates are strongly correlated with customer review scores — late deliveries consistently attract 1–2 star ratings.

Chapter 05 — Customer Churn

Over 95% of customers never returned

Olist faces a severe one-time buyer problem. The vast majority of customers placed exactly one order and never returned — making retention the platform's biggest growth lever. Loyal customers, while rare, spend significantly more on average over their lifetime.
